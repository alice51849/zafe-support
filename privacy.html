Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-04

Zafe is designed as a local-first private photo and video vault. This policy explains what Zafe does and does not collect.

1. Data Not Collected

Zafe does not collect personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no developer-operated servers. We do not receive your photos, videos, vault contents, passcode, device identifiers, usage history, payment card details, or location history.

2. Photos and videos stay encrypted on your device

When you import photos or videos into Zafe, they are stored in an AES-256 encrypted space on your iPhone or iPad. Your private content does not leave the device through Zafe, and the developer cannot view it.

3. No account, ads, analytics, tracking, or servers

Zafe does not ask you to sign in, does not build a profile about you, does not show ads, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. The app does not connect to a server for your vault content.

4. Permissions

Zafe requests only the permissions needed for features you use: Photo Library to import the items you choose and optionally remove originals after import; Face ID / Touch ID to unlock the vault; Camera only for the optional intruder selfie in Break-in log; and Location only if you choose to attach a location to an intruder log entry. Permission-related data is stored only on device.

5. Local app data

Zafe may store vault metadata, settings, lock preferences, Pro entitlement status, decoy vault data, and optional intruder log entries locally on your device so the app can function. This information is not sent to us.

6. Purchases

Zafe Pro is a one-time, non-subscription In-App Purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

7. Deleting data

You can delete content from within Zafe or remove the app from your device. Because Zafe is local-first and does not maintain a server copy, deleting local content removes it from the app’s local vault.

8. Children’s privacy

Zafe does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about privacy or support, contact us at hourstag.app@gmail.com.
