Zafe Privacy Policy

Zafe Privacy Policy

Protect photos, videos, files and notes on your device with Face ID and a password-protected backup.

Security

Media, file and note payloads, together with thumbnails, are encrypted on device with CryptoKit AES-GCM. SwiftData metadata—such as names, original filenames, dates and optional coordinates—is protected by iOS Data Protection and is not described as app-level AES encryption.

Purchases

Zafe has no developer-operated server. When Zafe checks Pro access, or when you open the purchase screen, buy or restore, Apple StoreKit receives the product ID, storefront and purchase/restore transaction data to show the local price and process entitlement. Apple retains that data under its policies. Zafe keeps only local entitlement state; the developer receives no card details and does not share this data.

Location

Intruder camera and location are off by default and each requires explicit opt-in. After intruder capture is enabled, optional location uses iOS Location Services to add coordinates to the local log. When you view a located entry, CLGeocoder sends those coordinates to Apple to return a place label. Apple Maps receives the selected coordinates only after you tap Open in Maps. Coordinates remain in local SwiftData until you delete the entry or app; Zafe sends none of this to a developer-operated server or other parties.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Zafe has no account, ads, analytics or tracking, and no private browser, embedded web content, remote config, VPN or NetworkExtension.

Information kept on your device

Vault items, thumbnails, SwiftData metadata and optional intruder records remain locally while needed for the features you use. You control deletion of items and log entries; exported backups remain wherever you save them. Apple-service data is retained by Apple under its policies, not by the developer.
