FAQ

Support

What is Zafe?

Zafe is a private photo and video vault for iPhone and iPad. It protects imported media with Face ID / Touch ID, a 6-digit passcode, and AES-256 encrypted on-device storage.

Are my photos or videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Zafe is local-first, has no account system, and does not connect to any server for your vault content. The developer cannot see your photos or videos.

What is included for free?

Core features include creating one album, importing photos and videos, viewing and playing vault content, Face ID / passcode lock, auto-lock, and an app switcher privacy screen.

What does Zafe Pro unlock? Is it a subscription?

Zafe Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited albums and items, Decoy vault, Break-in log, Private browser downloads, and alternate app icons.

What is Decoy vault?

With Zafe Pro, you can set a decoy passcode that opens a separate harmless vault, keeping your real vault hidden behind your main passcode.

How does Break-in log work?

If enabled, Zafe can record the time of a wrong passcode attempt and optionally capture a front-camera photo. Location is optional and only used if you allow it for that log entry.

Why does Zafe ask for permissions?

Photo Library is used to import items you choose and optionally remove originals. Face ID / Touch ID unlocks the vault. Camera is only for optional intruder selfies. Location is only optional for Break-in log entries.

What if I forget my passcode?

For privacy, Zafe does not have a developer-accessible account or recovery server. Keep your passcode safe; the developer cannot unlock or view your vault.
