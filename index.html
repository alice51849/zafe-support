Zafe

Clear on-device privacy

Protect photos, videos, files and notes on your device with Face ID and a password-protected backup.

App StoreView support

Lock photos, files & notes

Free includes 1 album, up to 10 items, Secure Notes, auto-lock, and Face ID or a passcode.

Security

Media, file and note payloads, together with thumbnails, are encrypted on device with CryptoKit AES-GCM. SwiftData metadata—such as names, original filenames, dates and optional coordinates—is protected by iOS Data Protection and is not described as app-level AES encryption.

Purchases

Upgrade to Pro with a one-time purchase to unlock unlimited albums and items, a secondary safety vault (Decoy Vault) with its own passcode, Intruder Capture that is off by default and works only after explicit opt-in and camera permission—with time and optional location—and alternate icons that are clearly branded Zafe.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Zafe has no account, ads, analytics or tracking, and no private browser, embedded web content, remote config, VPN or NetworkExtension.
